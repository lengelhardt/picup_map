# PICUP Across the Map

An interactive, animated map of where the Partnership for Integration of Computation into Undergraduate Physics (PICUP) has gathered, from 2016 to now: workshops, regional DICE events and conference sessions across the United States, alongside the online webinars and virtual workshops.

**View it in your browser:** https://lengelhardt.github.io/picup_map/

Nothing to install. To view it offline, download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. An internet connection is needed for the map libraries (d3 and topojson) and the web fonts.

## What it shows

- **Map:** one marker per host city, appearing as the timeline reaches its first event. A badge counts repeat visits, and dashed rings mark upcoming events. Nearby cities are nudged apart and joined to their true location by a thin line.
- **Timeline:** press play to animate from January 2016 through the events scheduled for late 2026, or drag the slider to any date. Ticks mark in-person events (teal) and online events (green).
- **Running totals:** in-person events, places and online events up to the chosen date.
- **Side panels:** the most recent in-person event and the latest online events.

Hover over, or tap, a marker to list the events held there.

## Data

Events are taken from the PICUP Events page at [compadre.org/picup/events](https://www.compadre.org/picup/events/), read September 26, 2026. The page is a snapshot and does not update itself.
